1 km forecast maps

Each 1 km square is estimated from the weather forecast, CAMS and the measured PM2.5 of nearby stations. Between stations the map is less accurate than the station forecasts (typical error about 13 µg/m³ against about 7 µg/m³ at stations).

How the forecast works

A LightGBM model starts from each station's measured PM2.5 of the last two days and predicts how much it will change, using the ECMWF IFS 9 km weather forecast (boundary-layer height, rain, humidity, temperature, wind), the CAMS aerosol forecast, the place and the season. Tested on January–August 2026 at 14 stations, its typical error was 6.6 / 8.5 / 9.6 µg/m³ for today / tomorrow / the day after, against 7.5 / 10.6 / 12.9 for "same as yesterday".

Data: CPCB CAAQMS (via data.gov.in), ECMWF IFS HRES (via Open-Meteo), Copernicus CAMS. Colours follow the CPCB PM2.5 sub-index classes (24-h mean, µg/m³): Good 0–30, Satisfactory 31–60, Moderate 61–90, Poor 91–120, Very poor 121–250, Severe above 250.
